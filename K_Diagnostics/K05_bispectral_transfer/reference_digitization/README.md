# Source-derived reference figure readings

Six CSV traces (295 manually selected points) and a 192 x 192 heat-map
approximation were read from Figures 2 and 3 of Brown & Jorns,
Phys. Rev. Lett. 130, 115101 (2023), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.130.115101.
These are approximate digitizations of published graphics, **not author raw
data**, independent simulations or experimental results from this project.
The original paper images are not included here. See provenance.json.

Curve ordinates inherit the source axes and their frequency convention; do not
use the digitized dispersion slope as a calibrated group velocity. The heat
map inherits image resolution, clipping, annotations and grayscale conversion.
It contains no recoverable complex phase. Its title-overlay region was locally
interpolated. The CSV column names refer to the displayed source axes.

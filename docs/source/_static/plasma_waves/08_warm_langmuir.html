08 · 温度怎样改变波？08 · How does temperature change the wave?

把一小团电子压密Compress a small cloud of electrons

第 07 课中，电子受电场拉回，在固定离子背景中沿波的方向振荡。这种静电波叫作 Langmuir 波。电子有热运动时，还有什么力？In lesson 7 the electric field pulled electrons back along the wave against a fixed ion background. This electrostatic oscillation is a Langmuir wave. What force does thermal motion add?

电子向各个方向运动，穿过一小块截面时会传递动量。单位面积上的这种动量通量形成电子压强 p，单位 Pa。Electrons carry momentum across an imaginary surface as they move in random directions. This momentum flux gives the electron pressure p, measured in Pa.

沿波的方向，密集区压强升高。左侧压强向右升高，压力把电子推向左；右侧压强向右降低，电子被推向右。压强梯度也有恢复密度均匀的作用。Along the wave, pressure rises in a compressed region. On the left slope the pressure force points left, and on the right it points right. Both forces act to relieve the compression.

给冷模型加一项力Add a force to the cold model

保留第 07 课的一维小扰动、均匀背景与静电场。连续性方程和泊松方程仍然适用。Keep the one-dimensional small perturbation, uniform background and electrostatic field of lesson 7. Continuity and Poisson’s equation still apply.

电子质量为 mₑ [kg]，电荷为 −e [C]。电场扰动 E₁ [V/m] 原先单独决定平均速度扰动 v₁ [m/s] 的变化。An electron of mass mₑ [kg] and charge −e [C] feels the perturbed field E₁ [V/m]. In the cold model this force alone changes the mean velocity perturbation v₁ [m/s].

mₑ ∂ₜv₁ = −eE₁

每单位体积的压力是 −∂ₓp₁ [N/m³]。除以背景数密度 n₀ [m⁻³] 就得到每个电子分到的力。The pressure force per volume is −∂ₓp₁ [N/m³]. Divide by the background number density n₀ [m⁻³] to get the force per electron.

mₑ ∂ₜv₁ = −eE₁ − (1/n₀) ∂ₓp₁

同样的压缩会增加多少压强？How much does compression raise pressure?

Tₑ 是平衡电子温度 [K]，kB 是玻尔兹曼常数 [J/K]。背景压强为 p₀ = n₀ kBTₑ。Tₑ is the equilibrium electron temperature [K] and kB is Boltzmann’s constant [J/K]. The equilibrium pressure is p₀ = n₀ kBTₑ.

压缩沿传播方向发生。若一个振荡周期内沿该方向的热量交换可以忽略，采用一维绝热关系，纵向压强随密度的三次方变化。Compression acts along the wave. When heat exchange along that direction can be neglected during an oscillation, the one-dimensional adiabatic law gives the longitudinal pressure.

p / p₀ = (n / n₀)³

写 n = n₀ + n₁，只保留 n₁/n₀ 的一次项。令 p₁ = p − p₀，温度就进了动量方程。Write n = n₀ + n₁ and keep only the term linear in n₁/n₀. Subtract p₀ to obtain the pressure perturbation that enters the momentum equation.

p / p₀ ≈ 1 + 3n₁/n₀
p₁ = 3kBTₑ n₁

把压强带回色散关系Put pressure back into the dispersion relation

第 07 课把压强对密度的响应写作 p₁ = mₑu²n₁，其中 u 的单位是 m/s。现在可以确定 u 的大小。Lesson 7 wrote the pressure response as p₁ = mₑu²n₁, where u is measured in m/s. The temperature now fixes its value.

比较两个压强表达式。定义单方向电子热速 vTe [m/s]，即该方向随机速度的均方根。Match the two pressure expressions. Define the one-direction thermal speed vTe [m/s] as the root-mean-square random velocity along the wave.

vTe² = kBTₑ / mₑ
u² = 3vTe²

将 u² 代入上一课的结果。ωpe 是同一密度下的冷电子等离子体角频率 [rad/s]，k 是波数 [rad/m]。这一长波热修正称为 Bohm–Gross 关系。Insert u² into the result of lesson 7. Here ωpe is the cold plasma angular frequency [rad/s] at the same density and k is the wavenumber [rad/m]. This long-wavelength thermal correction is the Bohm–Gross relation.

ω² = ωpe² + u²k²
ω² ≈ ωpe² + 3k²vTe²

固定密度，对照两条曲线Compare both curves at one density

先选定一个 k，再提高温度。预测两条曲线在哪一端最容易分开。Pick a wavenumber, then raise the temperature. Predict which end of the curves will separate most.

两点取同一波数。金色虚线是冷模型，绿色实线是温模型。横移读数点，可比较不同波长的热修正。The two markers share a wavenumber. Gold dashes show the cold model and the solid green line shows the warm model. Move the markers to compare wavelengths.

当 k 趋近于零时，两条曲线都趋于 ωpe。波长越短，同一密度扰动产生的压强变化越集中，压强梯度越大。这里取正频率分支。As k approaches zero, both curves approach ωpe. The same compression over a shorter wavelength produces a steeper pressure gradient. The graph shows the positive-frequency branch.

波数加倍，哪个量变成四倍？Double k. Which quantity quadruples?

保持密度和温度不变，把 k 从 10 rad/m 调到 20 rad/m。先判断，再查看答案。At fixed density and temperature, move k from 10 to 20 rad/m. Make a prediction before revealing the result.

可比较 ω 本身，也可以比较频率平方相对于冷模型的增量。Compare the angular frequency itself with the excess of its square over the cold value.

Δ = ω² − ωpe²

四倍的是 Δ。ω 还包含冷模型的贡献，并且要开平方。使用长波近似时要求 kvTe/ωpe 远小于 1；图中这一比值最大约 0.20。It is Δ that quadruples. To find ω one must add the cold contribution and take a square root. The long-wave approximation requires kvTe/ωpe well below one. This ratio reaches about 0.20 in the plot.

Δ ≈ 3k²vTe²

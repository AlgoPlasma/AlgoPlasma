12 · 如何看模拟中的 ω–k 谱图？12 · How do we read an ω–k spectrum?

从一列已知行波开始Start with one known traveling wave

像模拟输出一样，在许多位置、许多时刻保存电场 E(x,t) [V/m]。先用一个知道答案的信号试读。As in a simulation, save the field E(x,t) [V/m] at many positions and times. Start with a signal whose answer is known.

每一行是同一时刻的空间快照，向上是较晚的时刻。正负电场用不同颜色表示。随时间向右移动的波峰留下倾斜的条纹。Each row is a spatial snapshot, with later times higher up. The two colors indicate opposite field signs. A crest moving right leaves a sloping stripe.

输入振幅 A = 1 V/m，波数 k₁ = 12 rad/m，角频率 ω₁ = 1.015 × 10⁸ rad/s。能从记录中找回这对数吗？The input has amplitude A = 1 V/m, wavenumber k₁ = 12 rad/m and angular frequency ω₁ = 1.015 × 10⁸ rad/s. Can the record recover that pair?

E(x,t) = A cos(k₁x − ω₁t)

用每个候选波去比对记录Match the record against trial waves

挑一组候选 k 和 ω，把每个采样值乘上相反相位的复指数，再对全部位置和时刻取平均。Choose a trial k and ω. Multiply each sample by the complex exponential of opposite phase, then average over all positions and times.

xj 与 tl 分别是第 j 个位置 [m] 和第 l 个时刻 [s]，N 是时空采样点总数。C [V/m] 是这一对坐标对应的复系数。xj and tl are the j-th position [m] and l-th time [s]. N counts all space–time samples. C [V/m] is the complex coefficient at this coordinate pair.

C(k,ω) = (1/N) Σj Σl E(xj,tl) exp[−i(kxj − ωtl)]

匹配的分量相位抵消后会同向相加；不匹配时，不同采样点的贡献大多相消。对整张坐标网格做这件事，就是二维离散傅里叶变换。For a matching component the phase cancels and contributions add coherently. For a mismatch, contributions largely cancel. Repeating this across the coordinate grid gives the two-dimensional discrete Fourier transform.

读出一个亮点Read the coordinates of a bright spot

横轴是波数 k [rad/m]，纵轴是角频率 ω [10⁸ rad/s]。找出最亮的格子，向下、向左分别读数。The horizontal axis shows k [rad/m] and the vertical axis shows ω [10⁸ rad/s]. Find the brightest cell and read down and left.

格子亮度来自谱功率 P = |C|²。颜色按相对最强格子的分贝数 dB 着色：0 dB 最强，−20 dB 表示功率为其百分之一。Cell color encodes spectral power P = |C|² relative to the strongest cell. Zero dB is the maximum and −20 dB means one hundredth of its power.

dB = 10 log₁₀(P / Pmax)

峰值落在 (12, 1.015)。它找回了输入波。这里放大右行波所在的正 k、正 ω 区域；实信号在 (−k, −ω) 还有一个对称峰。The peak at (12, 1.015) recovers the input wave. We zoom in on positive k and positive ω for these right-going waves. A real signal also has a mirror peak at (−k, −ω).

再加一列较弱的波Add a weaker second wave

第二列波取 k₂ = 20 rad/m、ω₂ = 1.040 × 10⁸ rad/s。固定这两个数，只调第二列波的振幅。Add a second wave at k₂ = 20 rad/m and ω₂ = 1.040 × 10⁸ rad/s. Hold both coordinates fixed and vary its amplitude.

把第二列波的振幅调到第一列的一半。先判断第二个亮点的位置和颜色，随后调到零再比较。Set the second amplitude to half the first. Predict the location and color of the second spot, then compare with zero amplitude.

振幅减半时，该分量功率变为四分之一，约 −6.0 dB。坐标仍是输入的 k₂ 和 ω₂。振幅调到零时，该处分量消失。Half the amplitude gives one quarter of the power, about −6.0 dB. Its coordinates remain k₂ and ω₂. With zero amplitude that component disappears.

许多相邻亮点怎样连成带？How do neighboring peaks form a band?

把 5 到 24 rad/m 的二十个相邻波数叠加，每个分量的频率由第 10 课的温电子关系给出。对合成记录再次做同一变换。Superpose twenty neighboring wavenumbers from 5 to 24 rad/m. Give each the warm-electron frequency from lesson 10 and transform the combined record in exactly the same way.

一列波照亮一处，相邻波数都受到激发时，最亮的一串格子沿频率上升方向排成带。有限的记录时长还会把不在格点中心的频率分散到邻格。One wave lights up one location. With adjacent modes excited, their strongest cells line up into a rising band. A finite record also spreads an off-grid frequency into neighboring cells.

空间记录长 L = 2π m，时间记录长 Tᵣ ≈ 25.13 μs。格距决定可直接读出的精细程度。延长记录时间会缩小频率格距。The spatial record spans L = 2π m and the time record spans Tᵣ ≈ 25.13 μs. Their lengths set the grid spacing. A longer time record reduces the frequency spacing.

Δk = 2π/L = 1 rad/m
Δω = 2π/Tᵣ = 2.5 × 10⁵ rad/s

叠上理论线，再判断符合程度Overlay the prediction and compare

先在 k = 16 rad/m 处读出谱峰频率，再打开理论曲线。两者相差几个频率格？Read the peak at k = 16 rad/m, then turn on the theory curve. How many frequency bins separate them?

曲线由给定的背景密度和电子温度算出。颜色来自采样电场的变换。比较某个 k 处的峰值频率和曲线，读数精度受频率格距限制。Density and electron temperature determine the theoretical line. The sampled field determines the colors. Compare the peak and the curve at one k, accounting for the frequency-bin spacing.

ω(k) ≈ √(ωpe² + 3k²vTe²)

为什么曲线会经过暗区？本例只激发了 5 至 24 rad/m 的分量。理论给出模型允许的频率，亮度还取决于哪些分量实际出现，以及它们有多强。Why does the line pass through dark cells? Only modes from 5 to 24 rad/m were excited. Theory supplies the allowed frequencies. Brightness also depends on which modes are present and on their amplitudes.

应用到模拟时，先核对输出量、轴的单位、傅里叶符号和记录长度，再叠加与模拟参数相符的理论线。本例用理论频率生成输入，可用来校验读谱方法。For a simulation, check the recorded quantity, axis units, Fourier signs and record length before overlaying a prediction for its parameters. Here the input frequencies came from theory, providing a check of the analysis.

09 · 波为什么增长或衰减？09 · Why do waves grow or damp?

看探针记录的峰间距和峰高Measure the peak spacing and peak height

把探针放在 x = 0 m，记录电场 E [V/m]。播放后，沿时间轴观察一列越来越弱的振荡。Place a probe at x = 0 m and record the electric field E [V/m]. Play the trace and follow an oscillation as it fades.

相邻两个同向波峰的时间间隔给出周期。绿色曲线来回振荡，金色虚线勾出振幅随时间变化的边界。The time between successive positive crests gives the period. The green trace oscillates between the gold dashed envelopes.

一个周期过去，峰间距依旧，峰高已经减小。我们需要两个数分别描述这两种变化。One cycle later, the peak spacing is unchanged but the peak is lower. Two numbers can describe these changes separately.

允许角频率有一个虚部Allow an imaginary part in the frequency

沿用前几课的相位约定 kx − ωt。设 k 为实数 [rad/m]，x 用 m，t 用 s。Keep the phase convention kx − ωt. Take a real wavenumber k [rad/m], position x in metres and time t in seconds.

取一个实的初始振幅 A₀ [V/m]。复数表达式的实部给出实际电场，其中 i² = −1。Use a real initial amplitude A₀ [V/m]. The physical field is the real part of the complex expression, with i² = −1.

E(x,t) = Re{A₀ exp[i(kx − ωt)]}

写成复频率。实部 ωᵣ 是振荡角频率 [rad/s]；虚部 γ 是振幅的增长率 [s⁻¹]。二者乘以时间后都能进入无量纲指数。Write the complex frequency in terms of the oscillation angular frequency ωᵣ [rad/s] and amplitude growth rate γ [s⁻¹]. Multiplication by time makes each exponent dimensionless.

ω = ωᵣ + iγ

指数里的负号去了哪里？Follow the signs in the exponent

把复频率代回相位，再用外面的 i 乘括号里的每一项。Substitute the complex frequency, then multiply each term in the parentheses by the outer i.

先展开 −ωt。Expand −ωt first.

i[kx − (ωᵣ + iγ)t]
= i[kx − ωᵣt − iγt]

因为 −i² = +1，含 γ 的项变成实数。指数相加对应指数函数相乘。Because −i² = +1, the term containing γ is real. A sum in the exponent becomes a product of exponentials.

i(kx − ωᵣt) + γt
exp[i(kx − ωt)] = eγt exp[i(kx − ωᵣt)]

取实部。括号外的实因子给出随时间变化的振幅。Take the real part. The real factor multiplying the cosine sets the amplitude.

E = A₀ eγt cos(kx − ωᵣt)

只改变振荡快慢Change the oscillation rate alone

让 γ = 0 s⁻¹，包络保持水平。将 ωᵣ 从 4 rad/s 提高到 8 rad/s。Set γ = 0 s⁻¹ so the envelope is level. Increase ωᵣ from 4 to 8 rad/s.

拖动观察时间或播放记录，数一数同一段时间内有多少个周期。Scrub or play the trace and count cycles in the same time interval.

T = 2π / ωᵣ

角频率加倍，周期从约 1.57 s 缩短到 0.79 s。两个设置下，电场都在 ±1 V/m 之间振荡。Doubling angular frequency shortens the period from about 1.57 s to 0.79 s. Both traces oscillate between ±1 V/m.

只改变振幅的增长或衰减Change the envelope alone

固定 ωᵣ = 8 rad/s，先试负、零、正三种 γ。关注金色包络和相邻过零点。Keep ωᵣ = 8 rad/s. Try negative, zero and positive γ. Watch the gold envelope and the spacing of successive zero crossings.

γ > 0 时包络张开，γ = 0 时保持水平，γ < 0 时收拢。任意相同时间间隔 Δt [s] 内，振幅都会乘上同一个因子。For positive γ the envelope opens, at zero it stays level, and for negative γ it narrows. Every equal interval Δt [s] multiplies the amplitude by the same factor.

A(t + Δt) / A(t) = eγΔt

若 γ = −0.30 s⁻¹，约 3.33 s 后振幅降至原来的 1/e。余弦的过零时刻仍由相位决定。At γ = −0.30 s⁻¹ the amplitude falls to 1/e of its starting value in about 3.33 s. The phase still determines the zero crossings.

τ = 1 / |γ|

能量流向哪里？Where does the wave energy go?

振幅变化意味着有能量交换。复频率记录变化的速度；找出交换的原因还要看等离子体的运动。A changing amplitude signals an energy exchange. The complex frequency measures the rate. The particle motion explains the exchange.

电子与波的电场可以相互做功。若电子平均从波中获得能量，波就衰减；若有定向运动的电子持续把能量交给波，波可以增长。Electrons and the wave field exchange energy through work. The wave damps when electrons gain energy from it on average. A drifting electron population can instead supply energy and drive wave growth.

试读一条记录：同向过零的间隔仍为 1 s，而包络每过 2 s 减半。ωᵣ 和 γ 各是多少？Read a trace whose same-direction zero crossings are 1 s apart and whose envelope halves every 2 s. What are ωᵣ and γ?

用周期求实部，用振幅比求虚部。负号同我们采用的相位约定一致。Use the period for the real part and the amplitude ratio for the imaginary part. The negative sign is consistent with our phase convention.

ωᵣ = 2π / (1 s) ≈ 6.28 rad/s
γ = ln(1/2) / (2 s) ≈ −0.347 s⁻¹
